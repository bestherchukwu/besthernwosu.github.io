INFRASTRUCTURE ARCHITECTURE

Enterprise Electricity Distribution Infrastructure Design

A single infrastructure design project supporting AEDC, BEDC and EKEDC.

Overview

Designed and supported enterprise infrastructure for critical electricity distribution applications, addressing availability, scalability, security, monitoring, capacity and disaster recovery.

Responsibilities

  • Infrastructure architecture and server sizing.
  • Cloud, Linux, database and network design.
  • Production and DR infrastructure planning.
  • Monitoring and alerting requirements.
  • Capacity planning and resource assessment.
  • Production troubleshooting and documentation.

Outcome

Established resilient infrastructure designs capable of supporting critical business applications with clear production, monitoring, capacity and recovery requirements.
